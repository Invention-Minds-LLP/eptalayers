## Users

**Primary:** IT and infrastructure decision-makers (CIOs, IT heads, infra managers) at mid-to-large enterprises in India, including the India offices and campuses of global companies. They come to the site to evaluate a partner who can design, supply, implement and then run their network and IT infrastructure. What they fear most is downtime and a fragmented set of vendors. They judge a partner on technical credibility, business continuity and long-term support.

**Secondary:** Job seekers (Careers page) and readers of the blog ("Epta Insights").

## Product Purpose

Epta Layers (Epta Layers Pvt. Ltd., Bangalore) is a single-point enterprise solutions provider for networking and communication infrastructure. It works with world-leading OEMs to deliver tailor-made solutions and covers the whole lifecycle: assessment and a preliminary blueprint of the customer's business, architecture design, hardware supply, implementation, post-implementation support, and 24x7 operations through its NOC.

The site exists to win enterprise infrastructure engagements. It succeeds when a qualified decision-maker trusts Epta enough to start a conversation: a contact-form inquiry, a NOC service inquiry, a security assessment request, or a call.

Vision: to provide the best IT infrastructure solutions, help customers improve business continuity, and become the most reliable technology partner to enterprise customers.
Mission: to be the point solutions team for all IT infrastructure needs, from implementation to business continuity.

## Positioning

- **OSI-rooted.** "Epta" is Greek for seven. The company's service framework is built on the 7 layers of the OSI model (Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application). Epta works through the layers to understand the customer's line of business before designing a solution, and it focuses on problem areas without losing sight of business KPIs. The name, the framework and the claim "security embedded in every layer" all come from this.
- **Single point of accountability.** One partner across network, security, collaboration, data center and operations, instead of fragmented multi-vendor setups. This is the recurring pain in the case studies.
- **Extended arm of OEMs.** Epta implements OEM solutions (OEM-certified implementations) and offers long-term support, while designing vendor-agnostically around the customer's needs.
- **LAER delivery model (Land–Adopt–Expand–Renew).** A customer-success framework that tracks KPIs for implementation, training and value realization. The content calls it proprietary.
- **Business continuity is the core.** "Your business operations never see the stop sign." The content's own analogy: Epta is like the chief engineer of a communications satellite, who integrates every sub-system and stays at the controls 24x7 so the signal never goes dark.

## Product Principles

1. **Continuity over novelty.** Every claim and flow should reassure a buyer who fears downtime. Reliability and long-term support outrank flash.
2. **The seven layers are the organizing idea, not decoration.** The OSI framework explains how Epta thinks, and the structure and storytelling should make that method legible.
3. **One partner, whole lifecycle.** Keep the single-point, design-to-operations story coherent across all seven solution areas rather than presenting them as disconnected services.
4. **Proof must be real.** Use only the evidence on hand: anonymized case studies, the single NOC quote, and OEM partners once supplied. Leave an empty slot empty rather than fill it with invention.
5. **Every page leads to a conversation.** Each solution page ends in a specific, relevant inquiry path, and contact details are always easy to reach.
