# Surface brief: Home B (alternative homepage)

Scope: `/home-b`, an alternative to the current homepage for client comparison. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Light only (client dislikes dark themes).
Audience: IT/infra decision-makers at Indian enterprises evaluating one accountable partner.
Action: request an assessment / talk to an engineer. Proof: three anonymized case studies (90–95% Wi-Fi accuracy for 1,500+ users; phases 2 & 3 greenlit across 4 sites; 40+-country engineering client) and one NOC quote. Benefits are shown qualitatively and as honest arithmetic from the visitor's own inputs; no invented savings, ROI or uptime figures.
Memorable moment: the seven OSI layers drawing themselves onto an office floor plan, then a Before ↔ With Epta scrub that resolves each room's scenario.
Per-section scenarios: seven rooms, seven scenarios (reception guests, open office Wi-Fi, conference calls, server room, NOC desk overnight, branch link, procurement and renewals), each tied to the layer and solution that resolves it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your office, surveyed layer by layer: the floor plan is the page. Refuses the light corporate default of office photo hero, benefit-icon cards and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Architectural site-survey sheet. Drafting white #F7F8F6 ground, ink #16191C plan linework at hairline weights, room tags (A-101…) in Barlow Condensed caps, Barlow for text. The seven OSI layers are CAD layers drawn in the logo's seven brand steps (L1 pink #FE046F to L7 orange #FF5F27). Status stays apart: amber fault pins before, green resolved stamps after, heatmap coverage green. No photos, no cards, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor watches Epta's work draw onto a building, recognises their own office's problems room by room, scrubs to see each one resolved, sees the honest numbers for their own setup, then requests an assessment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 4/12: H1, one-line sub, one orange "Request an assessment" key, and below it the CAD layer panel L7–L1 with toggles. Right 8/12: the floor plan (building + branch + cloud) with the layers switching on L1→L7 in sequence. Plan sheet title block bottom-right.

FORM: Wi-Fi site-survey floor plan with CAD layer panel, candidate 6 of 7; seed key 57e9e68a. Raises: one orange key per viewport plus a keyboard-operable Before ↔ With Epta scrubber (Creator hardware bench); every fault pin traced to its countering layer and solution (Tensegrity); measured case results set solid, illustrative plan data marked (Provenance ribbon); addressable room references #A-101… (Teletext). Signature interaction: layers drawing onto the plan; motion grammar: strokes draw along their path, rooms focus by zoom, states crossfade with the scrubber.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
