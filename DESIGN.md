# Design System: Epta Layers

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Field Document, In Daylight"**

Epta's pages are working documents an engineer would hand a client: paper, ink, ruled lines and the logo's seven steps, always on a light ground. One brand runs two surface worlds that share every brand and status decision but differ in the kind of document they are.

**World A, "The Signed Test Report"** (Homepage `/`, the shared shell, status pages). A continuous field certification report: cool report-white sheets on a desk ground, title blocks over heavy ink rules, hairline-ruled rows, measured values in a monospace, PASS verdicts, a folio closing every section. Archivo is worked across its width axis; Martian Mono is the meter readout. Diagrams are cable-certification instruments: wiremap, limit-line plot, scope console, failover line.

**World B, "The Site Survey"** (Homepage `/home-b`, under `body.theme-b`). An architect's survey drawing of the client's own floor: drafting-white paper, pure-white sheets edged in ink, Barlow and Barlow Condensed like CAD annotation. The signature is the floor plan: the seven OSI layers are CAD layers drawn onto the floor in the brand steps, with on-plan layer tags, fault pins resolving to green stamps, a coverage heatmap, OEM callouts, a legend and a title block, and revision captions (Rev A–F) as the engagement advances.

Both worlds are flat, light and evidence-led. Depth is sheets on paper, never shadow. Brand colour names layers and invites action; status colour (green, red, amber) reports results and never borrows brand. Each further page (solutions, case studies, story, careers, contact, blog, legal) is written as another document in one of the two worlds, with its own subject-specific scenario built from that world's materials.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light only: every band, panel, header and footer sits on ground, paper or sheet. No dark panels anywhere.
- The client logo (ink-wordmark lockup) and its seven orange-to-pink steps, mapped to the seven OSI layers, shared by both worlds.
- World A: Archivo across its width axis, Martian Mono for measured values, ruled report sheets, verdict chips, folios.
- World B: Barlow and Barlow Condensed, white drafting sheets on drafting-white paper, a CAD floor plan with layers drawn along their path.
- Status colours (pass green, limit red, fault amber) kept apart from brand.
- Flat depth: sheets edged in 1px rule with a 6px ink top edge, overlapping, never floating.

## Colors

A daylight palette of paper, rule and ink, stamped with a seven-step orange-to-pink brand ramp, with status colours held apart.

### Primary
- **Brand Steps 1–7** (brand-1 orange through brand-7 pink): the logo's seven bars, mapped top-down to the OSI layers: brand-1 is L7 Application, brand-7 is L1 Physical. In A they colour the solutions layer bars and the dots on the stacked OSI sheets; in B they are the seven CAD layer strokes, their on-plan tags and the hero layer-switch swatches. Brand-1 also draws the header nav underline, text-link underlines, the active step on the B rail, the active chapter numeral, the B scrubber progress, live traffic dots on the plan, and the rule and tint of the A row under test (probe-tint). Brand-7 sets the large Greek numeral and the LAER value trace in A.
- **Accent Fill** (brand-1, hover brand-3): the primary button in both worlds, with ink text.
- **Accent** (accent, deep pink): link hover colour, focus rings, caret, selection, arrow and row hover cues, contact icons. **Accent Deep** (accent-deep) is its darker partner. Focus-tint (brand-1 at 7%) fills a focused room on the B plan.

### Secondary
- **Pass Green** (pass): the verdict and the resolved state. Fills the A PASS / SIGNED OFF chip, gauge bands and the A failover service line; in B, resolved stamps, "ok" room dots, the incident dot once handled, and the coverage heatmap. **Pass Ink** (pass-ink) sets passed codes, recorded figures, checks, NOC traces and the live dot on light grounds.

### Tertiary
- **Limit Red** (limit): form errors only. The invalid-field underline, its message, and the status page's error header. A true red, deliberately distant from brand pink.
- **Fault Amber** (fault): fills for alarms and unresolved problems: B fault pins and fault dots, dead-zone hatching, the incident dot before it is handled. **Fault Ink** (fault-ink) is amber for strokes and text on light: NOC threat spikes, the A failover fault mark and its label, dead-zone outlines and tags, the incident ring.

### Neutral
- **Desk Ground** (ground): World A page body, hero and NOC bands, empty layer-bar cells.
- **Drafting White** (paper-b): World B page body and alternating bands (story, numbers); replaces ground under `body.theme-b`.
- **Report White** (sheet): A report sheets, the solutions log band, continuity band, header and footer bars, the A NOC console face, selected file tabs.
- **Sheet White** (sheet-white): B drafting sheets, the plan's floor, the schedule table, focused room rows, scrubber thumbs, the white reveal masks that draw dashed plan strokes in, and white text on ink tags.
- **Sheet Under / Sheet Deep** (sheet-under, sheet-deep): A sheets beneath (hover fills, unselected tabs, alternating stacked sheets, gauge tracks).
- **Rule / Rule Strong** (rule, rule-strong): hairlines between rows, sheet edges, inactive rail steps; stronger rules for table heads, field underlines, ghost button edges, placeholders, plan hatching and idle chapter numerals.
- **Plan Furniture** (plan-furniture): the faint line weight for desks and fixtures on the B plan only.
- **Ink / Ink 2 / Ink 3** (ink, ink-2, ink-3): headings, heavy rules, 6px sheet top edges, button text, plan walls and devices; support text and the LAER KPI target line; labels, codes, folios and plan room IDs.
- **Graphite 950 / 800 / 600, On-Graphite** (graphite-950, graphite-800, graphite-600, on-graphite): small instrument parts only, never surfaces: text on the pass chip, the wiremap's pin blocks and their numerals, the scrollbar thumb.

### Cable diagram colours
- **Wire Orange / Green / Blue / Brown** (wire-orange, wire-green, wire-blue, wire-brown), solid or white-striped and outlined in wire-outline: the T568B pair colours, inside the A wiremap only.

### Named Rules
**The Daylight Rule.** No Epta surface uses a dark panel, dark band, dark header or footer, or a dark theme. Every section sits on ground, drafting white or sheet. Graphite survives only as small instrument parts (chip text, connector pins, the scrollbar thumb).

**The Seven Steps Rule.** The brand steps follow the logo: brand-1 orange at L7 on top, brand-7 pink at L1 at the foot. When brand colour encodes layers, in either world, each layer gets its own step and the order is never shuffled, reversed or interpolated.

**The Status-Apart Rule.** Brand colour never reports a result. Resolved and passed are green, form errors are the true red limit, alarms and unresolved faults are amber (fault fills, fault-ink strokes and text). A fault is never drawn in brand orange or pink, and a brand fill never sits where a verdict would.

**The Ink-Beside-Verdicts Rule.** Red-family link text next to a status reads as a failure. Links are ink with a 2px brand-1 underline and turn accent only on hover.

**The Wire-Colors-Are-Data Rule.** The wiremap's pair colours are the physical T568B cable code, scoped to cable diagrams. They are data, not palette, and never appear on buttons, text or section surfaces.

- **B Label** (Condensed 600, 0.8125rem, 0.08em, uppercase): fieldset legend, schedule heads, title block.
- **B Tag** (Condensed 600, 0.75rem, 0.08em, uppercase): "Recorded" provenance tags, rail numerals.
- **Plan type** (Condensed, in plan units that scale with the drawing): room names 18px 700, layer tags 16px 700, room IDs 15px 600 0.08em, OEM callouts 17px 700 0.05em, core, survey, incident and dead-zone tags 13px 700, renew stamps 11px 700.

### Named Rules
**The Measured-Only Mono Rule.** In World A, Martian Mono is for things a meter could print: IDs, codes, counts, timestamps, phone numbers, readouts. Sentences, headings and labels stay in Archivo.

**The Width-Axis Rule.** In World A, hierarchy is carried by width as much as size: heads at 105–112% width, body at 100%, labels at 75%.

**The Lettering Rule.** In World B, anything that would be lettered on a drawing (room IDs, layer tags, legend, title block, schedule heads, step rail) is Barlow Condensed; statements are Barlow. No third family.

**The One-World-Per-Page Rule.** A page is set in one world's type. Shared shell components inherit the page's `--font-sans`; don't mix Archivo heads and Barlow heads on one page.

## Layout

Both worlds use a centered wrap (max 1320px, fluid gutter clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px)) and asymmetric two-column grids.

**World A.** Mostly 5/12 copy against a 7/12 sheet or instrument, clamp(40px, 5–6vw, 80–96px) gaps. Section heads pair a wide headline (1.25fr) with a short summary (1fr), 48px above the content. Sections run 128px top / 48px bottom (112–120px on the solutions log, NOC and continuity bands) and close with a folio 72px below. At 1080px the hero stacks and the header collapses to a drawer; at 860px sections compress to 88px / 32px and grids stack; at 640px ruled tables reflow to two-line rows.

**World B.** A 4/12 copy column against an 8/12 plan in the hero; 7/12 plan against 5/12 text in the story and benefits sections, with the plan sheet sticky at 96px from the top while chapters or rooms scroll past (each chapter at least 72vh). Sections run 112px (72px at 860px and below) and alternate between page ground and drafting-white bands edged with 1px rules. At 1080px grids stack and the hero plan moves above the copy; sticky plans pin at 72px.

**The Folio Rule (A).** Every World A section ends with the folio: report ID, a 35%-opacity hairline, the section name in label type, and "nn / nn" sheet numbering. There are no eyebrows above headings.

**The Revision Rule (B).** World B marks progress on the drawing itself: a six-step rail above the plan and a "Rev A–F · Step" caption below it. There are no eyebrows above headings; the chapter numeral sits beside its title as a step index, not above it.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, in both worlds. Nothing floats and nothing casts light. Depth is paper on paper: a sheet edged in a 1px rule with a 6px ink top edge, stacked or offset sheets, tabs behind a file, a white drafting sheet on drafting-white paper. State changes are fills and rules: hover moves a row one tone, the row under test gets probe-tint with a 2px brand-1 foot rule, a focused room gets focus-tint.

### Named Rules
**The No-Soft-Light Rule.** No drop shadows, no blurred shadows, no gradient washes or glows. Hard-edged devices native to the instruments are allowed because they are rules, not light: an inset 2px foot rule on a probe row, a 1px scope graticule, white-striped wire pairs with a 1px inset outline, a hard-stop progress fill on a range track.

**The Heatmap-Is-Data Rule.** The only soft-edged fill in the system is the World B coverage heatmap: pass green fading radially from 38% to transparent, because it plots measured signal. It never appears outside a coverage plot and never as decoration.

**The Ink-Edge Rule.** A standalone sheet (report, drafting sheet, form, schedule, status) is announced by a 6px ink top edge and a 1px rule border, never by lifting it.

## Shapes

Square. World A sheets carry no radius when they have an ink top edge (the coverage sheet, form and status sheets) and 2px otherwise; controls take 3px; fields have no box at all. World B sheets, tables and tags are square-cornered; the layer-switch box takes 2px. Round forms are reserved for meaning: layer dots on A stacked sheets (10px), B layer swatches (12px), room-state dots (9px, 1.5px ink ring), the live indicator, range thumbs (white with a 2px ink ring), fault pins and resolved stamps on the plan, and pill-shaped on-plan layer tags. The scrollbar thumb is a 6px-radius pill. Borders do the structural work: 1px hairlines, 2px ink rules to open and close a sheet or list, 6px ink top edges.

### Motion
One easing, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Values settle; lines and layers draw once along their path when they enter view. World A's signature interaction is a layer-by-layer test run resolving each row to PASS; World B's is layers drawing onto the floor and the plan revising step by step as the reader scrolls, plus a before/with-Epta scrubber that crossfades pins to stamps and patchy coverage to even coverage. Only live instruments loop (NOC scope traces, the B incident cycle). Everything collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every Epta surface light: ground, drafting white, sheet or sheet-white.
- **Do** write each new page as a document in one world (a test report in A, a survey drawing in B), with its own subject-specific scenario built from that world's materials.
- **Do** colour layers by their brand step in both worlds: L7 brand-1 orange down to L1 brand-7 pink.
- **Do** put ink text on brand-1 fills, and make links ink with a 2px brand-1 underline.
- **Do** announce a standalone sheet with a 1px rule border and a 6px ink top edge.
- **Do** report results only in status colours: pass green for passed and resolved, limit red for form errors, amber for faults (fault fills, fault-ink strokes and text).
- **Do** set World A measured values, IDs and codes in Martian Mono with tabular, slashed-zero numerals, and close every A section with the folio.
- **Do** letter World B plan annotation, legends, title blocks and table heads in Barlow Condensed.
- **Do** label illustrative drawings as illustrative and tag real figures as "Recorded".
- **Do** mark empty content slots with dashed rule-strong placeholders that say what is awaited.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use dark panels, dark bands, a dark header or footer, or a dark theme on any Epta surface.
- **Don't** use drop shadows, blurred shadows, glows or gradient washes; the coverage heatmap is the only soft fill, and only on a coverage plot.
- **Don't** use brand orange or pink for a verdict, an error or an alarm.
- **Don't** set link text in a red-family colour beside a status; use ink with the brand-1 underline.
- **Don't** shuffle, reverse or interpolate the brand steps when they encode layers.
- **Don't** use wire pair colours outside cable diagrams.
- **Don't** mix World A and World B type on one page.
- **Don't** use a navy gradient hero, data-center photography, or a grid of icon service cards.
- **Don't** put eyebrow kickers above headings; A uses the folio, B uses the rail and revision caption.
- **Don't** round corners beyond 3px except for the meaning-bearing dots, pins, stamps, swatches, thumbs and layer tags listed in Shapes.
